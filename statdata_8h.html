typedef struct StatData StatData

Structure representing a single statistical data record.

Fields:

  • id: unique identifier (long)
  • count: integer count
  • cost: floating-point cost
  • primary: 1-bit flag (0 or 1)
  • mode: 3-bit value (0-7)
